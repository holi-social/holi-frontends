Community Guidelines

holi strives to bring out the best of each individual and group by creating a safe and welcoming place that fosters genuine human connection. As a general rule, we require that Spaces and content you create are lawful and respectful to others.

If you feel that you and your rights are not being respected please let us know by reporting the person or content directly via holi or sending us an email to support@holi.social.

Be kind

  • To increase empathy, clarity and effectiveness of communication on holi, we encourage you to practice non-violent communication.

  • Harassment, bullying, and threats of violence of any kind isn't allowed and degrading comments about things like race, religion, culture, sexual orientation, gender or identity will not be tolerated. Spaces and users that incite violence or that promote hate based on identity or vulnerability will be banned.

  • Respect the privacy of others and do not divulge personal or confidential information about others on holi.

Be authentic

  • Please use your real name. You may not impersonate other individuals or groups.

  • Only create spaces for groups that you personally belong to and have internal approval for.

  • Make sure you have copyright for content that you post. When posting images of others make sure you have their explicit consent. Content that infringes on others' trademarks or copyright will be removed.

  • We strive to keep holi a spam-free environment. Please only post content that is relevant and constructive to the discussion. Content deemed as spam will be removed.

Keep it legal

  • Any terrorist, extremist or hate groups are forbidden on holi. There is zero tolerance for content that might promote or legitimate violent extremism, terrorism, war or human exploitation.

  • It is strictly forbidden to initiate or carry out any illegal and prohibited activity on holi (including the promotion of criminal activities and facilitating or participating in cyber crime; buying, selling, or facilitating sales of illegal or regulated products).

Any behavior, activity, content, person or group that we believe is contrary to these Community Guidelines may be sanctioned (e.g., removed), either temporarily or permanently.

What to do in case something goes wrong?

Please flag any potential violation of the Community Guidelines by reporting the person or content directly via holi or sending us an email at support@holi.social. Our community team will review all reports, get back to you as soon as we can and take appropriate action.
