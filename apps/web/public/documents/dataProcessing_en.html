Data Processing Overview

Below we would like to give you an overview of data processing at holi.social. You can find detailed data protection information at https://holi.social/data-dignity.

Controller and contact

Holi Moli GmbH, Eifflerstraße 43, 22769 Hamburg, Germany, is responsible for data processing. If you have any questions about our data processing, please contact us at support@holi.social.

Processing purposes and legal bases

We may process your data for the following purposes:

  • Providing our services and ensuring IT security;

  • Creating and managing the profile and community functions

  • Managing and responding to enquiries, surveys and feedback

  • Measuring reach and optimising our services;

  • Personalisation of content and direct advertising;

  • Sending notifications and newsletters.

In this context, we may also read or store information on your end device. Most data processing is carried out on the basis of our legitimate interests or to fulfil a contract. Otherwise, we may obtain your consent.

Processed data

The following of your data may be processed:

  • Inventory and contract data;

  • Contact and communication data;

  • Profile, image, video and community data;

  • Identifiers and usage data.

Recipients of the data

We work with partners, such as IT service providers, to provide our services. We have concluded data protection agreements with these partners. We may also transfer your data outside the European Economic Area. In this case, we take appropriate measures to ensure an adequate level of data protection.

Storage period

We will delete your data as soon as the purpose for which it was collected no longer applies and contractual or statutory retention periods have expired.

Rights of data subjects

You have the rights to information about data processing, correction and deletion of your data, restriction of processing, transfer of your data and the right to object to data processing, revoke your consent and complain to a supervisory authority at any time in accordance with the GDPR.
