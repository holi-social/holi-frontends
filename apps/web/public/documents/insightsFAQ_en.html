What is the holi feed?

Here you'll find content that informs, stories that inspire, and practical hacks for your social engagement. For example:

  • News from the app: What's new in the world of holi?
    Find out here, and tell us what you think of the new tools and features!

  • Who is here already? Which organizations and initiatives are on holi?
    Here, we present them and their work on a regular basis. 

  • Impact hacks: Which form of enterprise suits your project? How do you build a community?
    We collect the best hacks from our partner organizations, for more impact in your engagement.

  • Insights: How do you inform yourself?
    Our Insights are thematic deep-dives that help you understand the big issues of our time. An Insight examines one topic from different perspectives. It is compiled by our journalistic partners.

Like what you see? Make sure to check back regularly! The holi feed is constantly evolving.
